# 🏸 Badminton Finder

A bookmarklet that lists every free badminton court at **Oxstalls Sports Park** and **GL1** in Gloucester in one place, so you don't have to click through the [booking site](https://flgloucester.gladstonego.cloud/book) venue by venue and day by day. You tap it while you're on the booking site. It only reads availability. It never logs in or books anything.

**Setup page:** https://jacki2ya.github.io/badminton/

## Set it up on a computer

1. Open the [setup page](https://jacki2ya.github.io/badminton/).
2. Show your bookmarks bar (`Ctrl+Shift+B`, or `⌘+Shift+B` on a Mac).
3. Drag the green **🏸 Badminton Finder** button onto the bookmarks bar.

## Set it up on a phone

1. Open the [setup page](https://jacki2ya.github.io/badminton/) on your phone and tap **Copy bookmarklet**. If that doesn't work, copy everything in the box under the button.
2. Bookmark the setup page, then edit that bookmark:
   - name it **Badminton**
   - replace the address with the text you copied.

If you've already set it up on a computer, you can let your browser sync the bookmark to your phone instead:
- **Chrome:** sign in to Chrome with the same account on both.
- **Safari:** turn on Safari in iCloud on your Mac and iPhone.

## Use it

1. Go to [flgloucester.gladstonego.cloud/book](https://flgloucester.gladstonego.cloud/book).
2. Open the bookmark:
   - **Computer:** click it on your bookmarks bar.
   - **iPhone (Safari):** tap the book icon, then **Badminton**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** type **Badminton** in the address bar and tap the bookmark when it appears. Picking it from the bookmarks list won't run it.
3. Filter by venue, day, or time (daytime, after 5pm, weekends).
4. Tap **Book ›** on a time to open it in the booking calendar, then book as usual.

Tap **↻** to refresh, or **×** to close the list.

When the finder is updated, set it up again to replace your bookmark, because the bookmark holds its own copy of the code.
